Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Cross My Heart is a browser-based crossword builder for creating personalized puzzles for weddings, birthdays, celebrations, and other occasions.

Information you enter

You may enter crossword clues, answers, names, event details, and other personalized content while using the crossword builder. This information is processed locally in your web browser. Cross My Heart does not send your crossword content to a server, and the crossword builder does not collect or store your clues, answers, or generated crossword data.

Theme preference

The website stores your selected visual theme in your browser’s local storage so it can remember your preference. This theme preference is not sent to Cross My Heart’s servers. You can remove this preference by clearing your browser’s site data.

No account required

You can use the crossword builder without creating an account, signing in, or submitting personal information.

Cookies and analytics

Cross My Heart does not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. The crossword builder does not use analytics or other tracking services to collect information about your crossword content.

Third-party services

Cross My Heart is hosted using Vercel. When you visit the website, Vercel may process limited technical information needed to deliver, maintain, and protect the site. This may include information such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested pages or files, request timestamps, and security-related logs. Vercel handles this information according to its own privacy policy.

The website uses Google Fonts to display certain typefaces. When the fonts load, your browser connects to Google’s servers to request the font files. Google may receive technical information associated with that request, such as your IP address, browser information, and the requested font resources. Google handles this information according to its own privacy policy.

Cross My Heart does not send your crossword clues, answers, names, event details, or generated puzzle content to Vercel, Google Fonts, or any other third-party service. This content is processed locally in your browser.

The crossword builder does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, external APIs, third-party storage, user accounts, or payment services.

Your crossword data

Because your crossword is processed in your browser, Cross My Heart does not have access to a copy of the crossword content you create. If you refresh the page, clear your browser data, change devices, or use a different browser, your crossword entries may be lost unless you save or export them using a feature provided by the website.

Children’s privacy

Cross My Heart does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into clues, answers, or event details.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website’s features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows when this policy was last updated.
